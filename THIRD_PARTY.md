# Third-party components and inputs

NumPy, SciPy and pymoo are external dependencies installed with pip, not vendored packages. Their licenses and citation requirements remain in force. The repository's scientific adaptation code does not relicense those libraries.

Original public FJSP files are not included. The recorded processing-input source is https://github.com/SchedulingLab/fjsp-instances/tree/ac4c3402312bfbeafcf4472d78be567d4e6b46ab . Consult its documentation and each benchmark's source before reuse. The author-defined transport augmentation does not convert these into independently published native-transport benchmarks. Supporting outcome tables are derived author-generated results.

`code/engine/standard_baseline_engineering_20260908/external22` is an internal experiment directory name, not a claim of external validation. `domain_adapter_engineering_20260908` is the specified author implementation/adaptation, not official source from the cited article. Reproduction scope and comparisons remain as stated in the paper.
